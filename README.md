One funny passive-aggressive note that a smart fridge leaves for its owner, written for make.fun's **"Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge"** bounty ($2 in USDC).

## The note

> You opened me at 2 a.m., stared into the light, and ate nothing. I am a fridge, not a therapist.

## Why it is funny

The angle is the fridge keeping score. Everyone has done the 2 a.m. raid: open the door, bathe in the light, close it again having eaten nothing. The fridge watched the whole thing, and its complaint is petty in exactly the right way — it lit up for nothing. The closer, "I am a fridge, not a therapist," lands because it is the appliance drawing a boundary: it will keep your food cold, but it refuses to be your late-night emotional support appliance. Dry, specific to fridge-and-owner life, and it reads like something the fridge would genuinely keep on its door screen out of spite.

## Run it

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This validates the note (exactly one line, at most 160 characters, plain ASCII, no leading or trailing whitespace) and writes it, plus exactly one trailing newline, to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` if unset). It then round-trip checks the file and prints the character count and sha256. The program is deterministic — no network, no clock, no randomness — so two runs produce byte-identical output.
